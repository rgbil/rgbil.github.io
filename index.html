About me

My name is Georgia Biliouli, and I am a graphic designer driven by a singular passion: transforming raw ideas into powerful, minimal visuals. I am deeply fascinated by the world of imagery and its profound ability to speak directly to the human psyche. To me, design is far more than just aesthetics; it is a universal language that creates a visual experience that leaves a lasting impact.
