Packaging · 2024

Anthos Botanicals

A packaging system for a herbal tea range that had to stretch from four products to forty without being redrawn.

Client
Anthos
Role
Packaging system
Year
2024
Category
Packaging

The range grows every season, so the design could not depend on illustration commissioned per product.

Instead the system is a fixed layout with two variables: a colour drawn from the plant itself, and a single silhouette. New products slot in without touching the template, which means the client can extend the range without coming back.
