Elaia Olive Oil

Bottle and label design for a single-estate olive oil from Messenia, sold mainly in northern Europe.

Client
Elaia
Role
Packaging, art direction
Year
2025
Category
Packaging

The oil is pressed from one grove and changes noticeably from year to year, so the label had to make the harvest year the most legible thing on the bottle.

A single band carries the year at a size usually reserved for the brand name. The rest of the label is set in one weight, and the green shifts each season to match that year's pressing.
