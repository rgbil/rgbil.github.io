Meraki Studio

Identity for an architecture practice that works almost entirely in reused buildings, where nothing starts from a blank page.

Client
Meraki Architects
Role
Visual identity
Year
2025
Category
Branding

An architecture studio that never demolishes needed an identity that could sit on top of what already exists rather than replace it.

The result is a system of overlays: a fixed grid, one typeface, and a mark that is always placed over an existing photograph rather than on a clean field.
